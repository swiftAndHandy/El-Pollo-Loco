Gameplay

Pepe tries to save his village from a chicken gone mad. To do so, he arms himself with Tabasco sauce. He can collect up to 20 coins on his mission. But he has to be careful:

He is attacked on his mission by the crazy chicken's henchmen. He defeats them by jumping on them or hitting them with Tabasco bottles. However, if he himself is hit by one of the chickens, he can no longer jump for a while due to the pain. He also needs a moment to be able to move again after landing on the ground.

If you manage to defeat the final boss, you can add your score to the leaderboard. The score is calculated from the number of henchmen defeated, the coins collected and the time taken to complete the level.
